Phase 1: Motivation & Problem

The Rapid Application Window Problem

In competitive software engineering hiring, high-value remote positions often receive over 200 applicants within their first 3 hours before candidate intake is automatically capped or closed. Job seekers were spending 2-3 hours daily manually refreshing dozens of fragmented company career pages and Hacker News threads.

Commercial alert services suffered from 4 to 8-hour email dispatch delays. Job Pulse was built to solve this latency by polling sources continuously and serving an instant, zero-latency dashboard.

Phase 2: Full-Stack Architecture

Engineered for Sub-Second Search

The backend crawler operates asynchronously in the background, polling 12+ RSS feeds, sanitizing incoming payloads, stripping tracking parameters, deduplicating IDs, and updating a cached database.
