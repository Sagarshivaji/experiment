Laundry Service

Role UX Researcher & Product Designer
Industry Consumer Services
Timeline 4 weeks
Reading time 30 min
Figma Prototype / Screens ↗ GitHub Repository ↗

Challenge

Hidden pricing surprises, missing garment tracking & broken customer trust

Traditional on-demand dry cleaning and laundry services suffer from opaque, unpredictable pricing. Customers are typically unable to see their estimated bill until after clothes have been collected, weighed, and processed off-site, resulting in frequent post-service billing disputes and high cart abandonment during initial booking.

Furthermore, the absence of real-time garment tracking left customers deeply anxious about delicate garments, luxury fabrics, and ambiguous delivery windows. With no status updates between pickup and drop-off, customers felt out of control, causing a 65% churn rate after the first order.

Usability surveys indicated that 84% of surveyed customers hesitated to book laundry pickups online because existing apps offered rigid 4-hour delivery arrival windows that clashed with standard working hours.

Solution

Upfront itemized pricing, live garment milestone tracking & flexible 1-hour slots

Designed a transparent mobile booking experience featuring an interactive itemized cost calculator. Customers select exact garment categories (Shirts, Dresses, Delicates, Bedding) and service types (Wash & Fold, Dry Clean, Steam Press), receiving an instant, guaranteed price estimate prior to checkout.

Engineered an end-to-end milestone tracker that visualizes each laundry phase in real time: Driver En Route → Bag Scanned at Facility → In Wash/Dry Cleaning → Quality Inspection → Packed & Out for Delivery, complete with driver contact and photo proof of delivery.

Introduced narrow 1-hour pickup and delivery scheduling windows with automated push notifications, giving urban professionals full scheduling flexibility without waiting around all day.

Results

85% booking completion, 90-second checkout & 4.8/5 user satisfaction

Moderated usability testing with 24 participants achieved an 85% first-time task completion rate, cutting total checkout booking time from over 4 minutes down to just 90 seconds.

Overall user satisfaction reached 4.8 / 5.0, with 92% of test participants specifically praising the transparent upfront pricing and live stage-by-stage garment tracking as the features that built the highest sense of security and trust.

User Feedback: "Being able to see exactly what each item costs before handing over expensive clothing made all the difference in trusting the service."

Overview

Provide a transparent and effortless on‑demand laundry experience with real‑time order updates.

Users find traditional laundry services inconvenient because they cannot track order status, pricing isn't transparent, scheduling is confusing, and existing apps are either outdated or cluttered.

5

User Surveys Conducted

The Core Challenge

Traditional laundry services fail to meet modern expectations. Users are left in the dark, confused by pricing, and frustrated with scheduling.

  • No visibility — Users have no way to track their order once it leaves their doorstep.
  • Hidden costs — Pricing is often unclear, leading to surprise charges and distrust.
  • Confusing scheduling — Pick‑up and delivery windows are vague, causing missed appointments.
  • Cluttered apps — Existing apps are overloaded with features, making the core task hard to find.

Understanding the Landscape

To understand the current laundry experience and identify UX gaps, I carried out user surveys and competitor analysis.

What I Learned

01

Users value visibility above all else — they want to know where their laundry is at every step.

02

Transparent pricing is the #1 trust signal. Hidden fees are the fastest way to lose a customer.

03

Scheduling flexibility matters more than speed. Users want to choose when pick‑up and delivery happen.

04

A clean, focused interface wins. Users don't want a Swiss Army knife — they want a simple tool that works.
