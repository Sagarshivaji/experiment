Timeline4 months

Challenge

Scattered prospect databases, manual data enrichment & slow pipeline velocity

Enterprise B2B sales development representatives (SDRs) and outbound recruiting teams lost between 15 to 20 hours per week manually researching, vetting, and copying prospect records across disjointed directories, LinkedIn profiles, and public registry portals. Without a unified scoring model, reps wasted time contacting stale or disqualified accounts.

Manual copy-pasting of contact details into legacy CRM systems produced high data decay rates, frequent duplicate records, and incomplete prospect profiles. The lack of standardized data hygiene created friction between marketing qualification and sales outreach, dragging overall lead-to-opportunity conversion below benchmark.

Furthermore, stringent enterprise data privacy, GDPR compliance, and client NDA requirements prevented teams from adopting off-the-shelf consumer scraping tools that risked leaking confidential organizational parameters.

Solution

An intelligent command center with real-time scoring & 1-click CRM synchronization

Designed Lead Generator, an enterprise intelligence workspace featuring high-density list management, sub-120ms keyboard-driven data navigation, and an automated multi-source enrichment pipeline that consolidates verified company, funding, and direct contact data into a unified pane.

Integrated an AI-assisted qualification engine that automatically scores incoming accounts against custom ideal customer profile (ICP) parameters, highlighting high-intent signals and eliminating unqualified prospects before outreach begins.

Engineered a seamless one-click CRM sync integration with automated deduplication and field mapping, enabling SDRs to push fully enriched prospect accounts into active sales cadences instantly without leaving their primary view.

Results

3x faster qualified discovery, 45% reduction in manual data entry & full NDA compliance

Qualified prospect discovery velocity accelerated by 3x, freeing SDRs to redirect more than 12 hours each week from administrative data entry directly into active outbound sales conversations.

Automated enrichment workflows reduced manual CRM field population by 45% while slashing duplicate contact occurrences to virtually zero across participating sales pods.

Enterprise Compliance: Built from the ground up to satisfy strict enterprise confidentiality and NDA data governance standards, ensuring zero client data exposure.

Overview

Lead Generator is an AI-powered enterprise platform designed to help sales and recruitment teams discover, qualify, and manage potential business leads from multiple data sources.

The goal was to reduce manual lead research by centralizing lead discovery, AI-assisted qualification, and pipeline management into a single workflow.

120ms

Maximum input response lag

Problem

Sales teams relied on multiple websites, spreadsheets, and CRM tools to identify and organize potential leads. Gathering company information, validating contacts, and tracking outreach required repetitive manual effort and resulted in fragmented workflows.

As lead volume increased, teams struggled to prioritize high-quality prospects, maintain accurate data, and collaborate efficiently across sales pipelines.

Design Details

The platform was designed around three core experiences:

  • AI Lead Discovery: Automatically identify and organize potential leads based on industry, location, company size, and business requirements.
  • Lead Qualification: Surface relevant company information, contact details, and qualification insights to help sales teams prioritize opportunities.
  • Pipeline Management: Provide a centralized dashboard for tracking lead status, s, outreach progress, and follow-up activities.

This approach simplified lead management while enabling teams to focus on meaningful sales conversations instead of manual data collection.

Results

After implementing the Lead Generator platform, sales teams reported significant improvements in lead management efficiency and data accuracy. The AI-assisted workflows reduced manual research time, allowing teams to focus on high-value interactions with prospects.
