Laundry Service — UX Case Study

End-to-end user research and product design for an on-demand laundry app — addressing pricing transparency, pickup scheduling, and live garment status tracking.

Phase 1: Discovery & Surveys

Understanding the Laundry Dilemma

Young working professionals and students in urban metros spend an average of 4.5 hours per week washing, drying, and ironing clothes. While on-demand laundry apps exist, users face severe hesitation due to three factors: unclear per-kg pricing, fear of lost garments, and erratic pickup times.

Phase 2: User Flows & Information Architecture

Transparent 3-Step Booking Journey

To eliminate checkout drop-offs, we mapped out a streamlined booking flow where users choose their service (Wash & Fold, Dry Cleaning, Steam Press), select garment estimates with instant pricing calculators, and pick precise 30-minute pickup windows.
