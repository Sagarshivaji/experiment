My Research Process

A systematic approach to gathering and synthesizing user data.

1. Define the Problem

Aligning with stakeholders to understand business goals, constraints, and formulating clear research questions.

2. Select Methods

Choosing between qualitative (interviews) or quantitative (surveys) methods based on the stage of the product lifecycle.

3. Synthesize & Share

Analyzing data using affinity mapping, creating user personas, and presenting actionable insights to the design team.
